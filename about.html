About Us

Built to be one reliable call, not three.

Emblem Consults Ltd is a Kampala-based company supplying IT consultancy, stationery and document management to offices, schools and institutions across Uganda.

Our Story

Why we operate across three business lines

Emblem Consults Ltd started from a simple observation: the offices we supported for IT also spent hours every month chasing separate vendors for stationery, and even more time fighting overflowing filing cabinets and misplaced paperwork. Rather than leave clients to manage that themselves, we built the capacity to handle all three — properly, not as a side hustle bolted onto our main business.

Today, IT consultancy, stationery supplies and document management are run as three equally resourced business lines, each with its own stock, suppliers and staff attention. A client calling about a printer network gets the same responsiveness as one asking us to digitise a decade of paper files.
